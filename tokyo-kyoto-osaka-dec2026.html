Book ahead & watch out for

Shibuya Sky
Timed entry every 20 minutes and the evening slots go first. Book the moment tickets open.
Shinkansen seats
6 December is a Sunday. Reserve rather than riding unreserved.
The weekend
5–6 December falls on Saturday and Sunday, which is Kyoto at its busiest, and early December still has late autumn colour in places.
Arashiyama at night
There is no illumination. The Hanatouro event ended in 2021 and came back as an October event, which is why the grove sits at 08:30 instead.
Otagi Nenbutsu-ji
Closed Wednesdays and Saturdays. Monday works — but if the plan shifts a day or two, this detour silently breaks.
Kyoto bus passes
The bus-only ¥700 day pass was discontinued in 2023. The ¥1,100 subway-and-bus pass is the one to buy.
Osaka Amazing Pass
¥3,500 including Osaka Castle entry — but on Day 4 you leave the valid zone for Nara, so check the sums before buying.
Kobe
It came off the plan when Day 3 became a Kyoto night. To put it back, swap it for Nara on Day 4: Kyoto → Kobe is about 50 minutes, and Kobe → Osaka another 35 in the evening.
Sunset and cold
Dark by ~16:30 in Tokyo and ~16:50 in Kansai; 5–12 °C. Bring a windproof layer, not only a warm one.
